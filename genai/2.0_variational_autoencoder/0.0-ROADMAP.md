# Variational Autoencoder Learning Roadmap

This directory implements the eight VAE variant units retained by the
companion reading guide. The scripts are algorithm lessons, not a production
training framework: each entry keeps its direct baseline, objective,
probability interface, gradient boundaries, and sampling
path visible. Shared network blocks and evaluation utilities live in
`dl_utils/vae/`.

The file groups encode the recommended learning order. They do not imply that
beta-VAE, cVAE, IWAE, or later branches accumulate all earlier changes.

## 1. Completed Baseline

| Lesson | Role |
|---|---|
| `0.1_autoencoder.py` | Deterministic reconstruction baseline |
| `1.0_vae.py` | Standard diagonal-Gaussian VAE and reparameterization |
| `1.1_vae_evaluation.py` | Posterior reconstruction versus prior generation |

These completed introductory scripts retain their original 256x256 face
setup. Steps 2-5 use compact 32x32 models; the discrete-tokenizer sequence in
steps 6-8 uses 128x128 aligned CelebA images. The beta-VAE lesson deliberately
reuses the introductory setup so its training objective is the only
algorithmic change from the standard VAE.

## 2. Step 1: Beta-VAE

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2.0_beta_vae.py` | `1.0_vae.py` | Scale the complete per-sample KL by beta while sharing data, model, optimizer, and training loop | A directly inspectable `D + beta * KL` objective |
| `2.1_beta_vae_evaluation.py` | Matched standard-VAE and beta-VAE checkpoints | Compare both models on the same images and prior samples | Rate-distortion, per-dimension KL, active units, reconstruction, and collapse diagnostics |

These capacity diagnostics do not establish semantic disentanglement because
the face dataset has no independent ground-truth generative factors.

## 3. Step 2: Conditional VAE

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `3.0_conditional_vae.py` | Standard VAE | Condition the posterior and decoder; use a standard normal prior | Conditional distortion/rate and per-class prior samples |
| `3.1_conditional_vae_evaluation.py` | cVAE training entry | Separate training-time and generation-time information paths | Condition compliance, within-condition diversity, and shuffled-condition checks |

The target image is available to the recognition posterior during training
but never to the prior used for generation.

## 4. Step 3: Importance-Weighted Autoencoder

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `4.0_iwae.py` | Standard VAE | Replace the one-sample ELBO with a stable per-example K-particle bound | K=1 versus K=5, ESS/K, and equal training epochs |
| `4.1_iwae_evaluation.py` | IWAE runs with different K | Re-evaluate every run with the same large-K estimator | Bound uncertainty, rate, active units, reconstruction, and prior samples |

K changes the objective estimator and compute budget, not the posterior or
decoder family.

## 5. Steps 4-5: Hierarchical and Ladder VAEs

| Step and lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 · `5.0_hierarchical_vae.py` | Standard VAE | Add a second latent, `p(z1 | z2)`, two KL terms, and top-down sampling | Per-layer rates, active units, generation order, and layer closing |
| 5 · `5.1_ladder_vae.py` | Two-level HVAE | Fuse bottom-up evidence with the matching top-down prior by precision | Matched generator/capacity comparison and per-layer utilization |
| `5.2_hierarchical_vae_evaluation.py` | Both retained hierarchy models | Intervene on one stochastic layer at a time | Posterior/prior replacement and layer-closing counterfactuals |

The two scripts share the same generative hierarchy so the Ladder comparison
isolates its inference-network increment.

## 6. Steps 6-7: VQ-VAE and FSQ

| Step and lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 · `6.0_vq_vae.py` | Autoencoder | Learned vector quantization, STE, codebook loss, commitment loss, and an 8x-compressed 128x128 image path | Reconstruction, active codes, entropy, frozen class-conditional PixelCNN prior, and generation |
| 7 · `6.1_fsq.py` | Matched VQ-VAE interface | Replace learned lookup and commitment loss with fixed scalar levels | Matched reconstruction, effective rate, token utilization, conditional prior NLL, and generation |
| `6.2_discrete_tokenizer_evaluation.py` | Both retained tokenizers | Compare tokenizer and downstream-prior behavior under one protocol | Distortion, effective rate, reconstruction distribution, and prior generation |

Tokenizer training and prior training are separate stages. Both tokenizers map
CelebA-128 images to 16x16 token grids without using class labels; only the
second-stage priors are conditioned on the binary Smiling attribute. Each
training entry runs both stages in order and saves the final tokenizer and
prior weights together for its companion evaluation.

All three discrete-tokenizer lessons use the official 162,770-image training
and 19,867-image validation partitions. They center-crop aligned faces to
178x178, resize to 128x128, and normalize to [-1, 1], without random flips.
Class indices 0 and 1 mean "Not smiling" and "Smiling" respectively.

## 7. Step 8: VQGAN

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `7.0_vqgan.py` | VQ-VAE plus completed GAN training skills | Train a 16x-compressed 128x128 tokenizer with frozen learned LPIPS, delayed PatchGAN, fixed adversarial weighting, and a class-conditional frozen-token Transformer prior | Two-optimizer isolation, reconstruction, token usage, and prior generation |
| `7.1_vqgan_evaluation.py` | Completed VQGAN stage artifacts | Reload the default tokenizer, discriminator, and prior on the validation split | Paired fidelity, reconstruction distribution, token rate, prior NLL, PatchGAN diagnostics, and complete generation |

Training uses learned LPIPS v0.1 with a frozen VGG trunk and a fixed
adversarial weight after the discriminator warm-up. The 16x compression
keeps the Transformer at an 8x8 token grid. Smiling labels condition the
prior rather than the tokenizer. The evaluation script reports the default
VQGAN system; it is a companion, not a ninth retained model.

## 8. Running the Teaching Sequence

Scripts 3.0-7.1 run without command-line arguments. Edit their top-level
constants to explore hyperparameters. The implementations emphasize the
algorithm and do not aim to reproduce paper results exactly.

- Run each training entry before its evaluation: 3.0 then 3.1; 4.0 then 4.1;
  both 5.0 and 5.1 before 5.2; both 6.0 and 6.1 before 6.2; 7.0 before 7.1.
- VQ-VAE, FSQ, and VQGAN train the tokenizer first, freeze it, and then train
  the token prior in the same run. Their final weight files belong together.
- Checkpoints contain final model weights and constructor settings needed
  for evaluation. Re-running training replaces that lesson's outputs.
- KL warm-up, free bits, straight-through gradients, causal masks, and
  memory-bounded IWAE evaluation remain visible where the algorithm needs them.
- Inception and LPIPS use pretrained weights. Projected Inception distances
  support comparisons within these lessons and are not canonical FID scores.
- The route ends after VQGAN and hands off to pixel-space DDPM. KL
  autoencoders and Latent Diffusion remain in `../3.0_diffusion_model/`.
